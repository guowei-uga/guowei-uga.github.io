Research Interests

  • Mathematical Foundations of AI and Data Science
    • Topology, geometry, and algebra: Mathematical representations of data; topological data analysis; topological deep learning; combinatorial commutative algebra; topological learning; evolutionary de Rham-Hodge theory; persistent spectral graph; persistent Laplacian; homotopy continuation; unified topological approach; persistent sheaf Laplacians; path methods; persistent hyperdigraph methods; Mayer methods; persistent Dirac methods; persistent commutative algebra.
    • Machine learning: Generative network complex; graph neural networks; multiscale manifold regularization; machine learning; multitask learning; autoencoders; transformers; UMAP-assisted K-means clustering; dimensionality reduction; CCP dimension reduction; interaction topology; persistent Jones polynomial; persistent Khovanov homology; Topo2seqTransformer.
  • Mathematical Foundations of Biosciences
    • Biomolecular modeling: Differential geometry-based solvation models; geometric algebra-based docking; persistent topology of biomolecules; variational multiscale models for ion channel transport; DFT of proton transport; curvature-based biomolecular surfaces; multiscale virus capsid modeling; geometric modeling of protein complexes.
    • Protein and genomic systems: Mechanoelectrical transducers; flexibility-rigidity index (FRI) for proteins; stochastic B-factor prediction; electrostatic binding analysis; Reeb graph-based protein pocket detection; genomic data; Cahn-Hilliard for mitochondria; geometric algebra for protein modeling; geometric topology modeling; Mayer topology modeling; topological phylogenetics.
  • Drug Discovery, Bioinformatics, and Quantitative Systems Pharmacology
    • Molecular prediction: Protein-ligand binding affinity; pose prediction; MathDL; virtual screening; partition coefficient; solubility; toxicity; scoring, ranking, docking, and screening.
    • Mutation and drug design: Protein folding stability changes upon mutation; protein-protein binding affinity; genotyping; automated generation of drug-like molecules; proteome-informed AI for drug adduction; deep mutational scanning; multiscale transformers.
  • Mathematical Virology and Biomedical Engineering
    • Virology: Virus evolution, infectivity, vaccine breakthrough, and antibody resistance.
    • Imaging and engineering: Geometric flow approaches to images and surfaces; PDE transform; image edge analysis by coupled PDEs; bioluminescence tomography.
  • Numerical Methods, Materials, and Dynamical Systems
    • Wavelet local spectral methods and applications: Matched interface and boundary (MIB) methods; multidomain interface problems; computational electromagnetics; computational fluid dynamics; Richtmyer-Meshkov instability; structural stability analysis.
    • Physics and dynamics: Nano-electronic devices; nanofluidic systems; quantum Boltzmann theory; transport theory; reaction rate theory; controlling chaos; controlling turbulence; controlling pattern formation.
