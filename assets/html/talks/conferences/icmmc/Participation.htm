Participation in this conference

This conference is partial supported by the National Science Foundation (NSF). The NSF fund will be primarily used to partially support junior attendees and participants from under-represented groups such as female researchers and minorities.

If you are interested in attending this conference and need more information, please feel free to email one of the organizers, chenhua@whu.edu.cn, wei@math.msu.edu, zjyang.math@whu.edu.cn, xxye@ualr.edu. We may request a research statement and a reference letter from your supervisor
