Welcome to MCMM 2009!

A major development in mathematical science in the 21st century will be its novel applications to biomedical engineering and biological science. Revolutionary opportunities have emerged for mathematically driven advances in biomedical research. The Department of Mathematics at Michigan State University has identified this interdisciplinary research as an area of focus. Our interdisciplinary program emphasizes collaborations among mathematical biological and engineering scientists. We feel strongly that it is time to organize a conference to bring together researchers from mathematics, engineering and biomedical sciences to exchange ideas and to foster collaborations. This conference will be interesting to researchers of all career stages and graduate students, who work or are interested in the mathematical analysis of images and surfaces, and their applications
