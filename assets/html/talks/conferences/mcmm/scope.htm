Welcome to MCMM 2009!

The conference will cover a wide range of topics in images and surfaces. Example topics include but are not limited to geometric flows, higher-order curvature flows, singularity formation and control, Mumford-Shah functional, level set, gradient flows for surface generations surface free energy minimization, wavelet and multiresolution analysis, cell imaging and cellular image analysis biomedical imaging, molecular imaging, bioluminescence imaging, fluorescent imaging, PET imaging, ultrasound imaging, MRI, the analysis of protein and membrane surfaces, image segmentation, computational anatomy, pattern recognition, video analysis and processing
