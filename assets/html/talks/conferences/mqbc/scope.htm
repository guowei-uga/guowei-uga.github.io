Welcome to MQBC 2006!

The conference will cover a wide range of topics in quantitative biology. Example topics include protein structure prediction; molecular dynamics; molecular surfaces; evolutionary genomics; mathematical approaches to the discovery of gene motifs; gene regulation; explanatory and predictive models of cellular growth and interaction; metabolic circuitry and dynamics; signal transduction and enzyme dynamics; phylogenetic analysis; pattern formation and control; biosensors; cancer formation and control, and neuroscience.
