GETTINGTHERE....

BY AIR

Commercial airlines

Mackinac Island is served by the Pellston, Michigan airport,12 miles south of Mackinaw City. Commercial air service to Pellston isavailable daily through Northwest Airlink in Detroit.

Mackinac island airport

The Mackinac Island Airport has a 3,500-foot lighted,paved runway, capable of accommodating private and charter aircraft. Formore information, please call 906-847-3231. Horse-drawn taxi service isavailable. Please call 906-847-3323.

Air taxi service

Air taxi service between the Mackinac Island Airport andboth the Pellston and St. Ignace airports is available 24 hours a day.For information, call Great Lakes Air at 906-643-7327.

BY CAR

From Detroit:
Detroit to Mackinaw City is 5 hours via I-75 North toexits 337, 338 or 339. Detroit to St. Ignace is 5 1/2 hours via I-75 Northto exits 344 or 348. Travel time includes a 15 minute ferry ride to MackinacIsland.

From Chicago:
Chicago to Mackinaw City is 8 hours via I-94 East passingMichigan City. Continue East until you reach I-196 (Exit 34). Go Northeaston I-196 passing by Holland. Go North on US-131 to MI-55. Go East on MI-55to US-27. Go North on US-27 to I-75 North. Go North on I-75 to MackinawCity. Travel time includes a 15 minute ferry ride to Mackinac Island.
Chicago to St. Ignace is 8 1/2 hours via I-94 North toI-96, North to 131, North to 31, North to I-75, North to exits 344 or 348.Travel time includes a 15 minute ferry ride to Mackinac Island.

From Cincinnati:
Cincinnati to Mackinaw City is 9 hours going North onI-75 to Toledo. Take I-475 North passing by Toledo. Go North on US-23 toMichigan. Take I-75 North (will connect in Flint) I-75 North to MackinawCity. Travel time includes a 15 minute ferry ride to Mackinac Island.

From Fort Wayne:
Fort Wayne to Mackinaw City is 7 hours going North onI-69 towards East Lansing. Go North on US-27 to I-75 North I-75 North toMackinaw City. Travel time includes a 15 minute ferry ride to MackinacIsland.
