Computational topology/geometry/graph
Biweekly Seminars

The past few years have witnessed the rapid development in Computational topology/geometry/graph and their applications in data science, biological science, engineering, technology, and mathematics. To foster their future development and train a new generation of researchers, we organize a biweekly virtual seminar series during the 2020-2021 academic year. Attendance is welcome.  Students are strongly encouraged to attend! A corresponding link for exercise:   TDA papers
