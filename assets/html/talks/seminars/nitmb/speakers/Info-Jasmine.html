Jasmine Foo's research focuses on mathematical models of cancer evolution and treatment response. She received her Ph.D. at Brown University and subsequently completed postdoctoral training at the Dana Farber Cancer Institute and Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center. She has been the recipient of an NSF CAREER Award, NCI Young Investigator Award, Fulbright Grant and a McKnight Land-Grant Professorship. She is currently a Professor at the School of Mathematics at the University of Minnesota.
